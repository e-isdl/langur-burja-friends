How to Play

1.Host toggles players on/off. Every active player bets each round.
2.On your turn, pick a stake — ₹5, ₹10 or ₹15 — then tap a symbol to bet on it.
3.Changed your mind? Tap ↶ Undo to take back the last bet.
4.When everyone has bet, hit 🎲 Roll Dice. The dice are shaken in a bowl and thrown. Wherever they land, that's the result.
KEY RULE: 1 match does NOT win. You need 2 or more dice showing your symbol to win.
Payouts:2 matches → 2× stake · 3 → 3× · 4 → 4× · 5 → 5× · 6 → 6× (plus your stake back).
Loss:0 or 1 match — you lose your stake.
